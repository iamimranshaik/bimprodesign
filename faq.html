Frequently asked

Before you enroll

Answers to what people usually ask before signing up.

Who is this course designed for?

This program is open to fresh graduates, diploma holders, and working professionals in Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, or Architecture fields.

Do you offer a job placement guarantee?

No. We focus on hard engineering competence and live consultant-level blueprints so you can clear interviews and manage projects confidently.

What are the computer requirements?

A minimum Intel i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 processor, 16 GB RAM, 4 GB dedicated graphics, and 512 GB SSD is recommended for Revit.

Are the classes recorded or live?

We provide 100% live interactive training sessions paired with intensive practical design and modeling lab work.
