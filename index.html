About the institute

Founded by engineers who still work on live projects

BIM PRO Design & Engineering Training Institute was founded by highly experienced professionals to nurture qualified intellectuals in the design and engineering industry. Our aim is to deliver high-quality professionals to a competitive market.

Our trainers bring hands-on experience across residential, commercial and large-scale infrastructure work, and stay involved through the whole training process — not just the first week.

Every discipline is taught through project-based learning: practical design exercises built around current industry standards, so students understand how real construction documentation actually works, and graduate ready to manage projects from day one.
